# CrownIQ research registry and synthetic acceptance cases

`packages/engine/src/research-registry.ts` defines versioned, market-specific research policies. Each lists a source priority, required/important/optional evidence kinds, a TTL in hours, and the GKR components each finding informs. Policy source identifiers are routing labels, not proof that a website was visited. `routeResearch` considers only matching event, player/team and market findings; it chooses fresh attributed evidence by source priority, reports stale and missing fields, and gives a deterministic research status: `PASS` for absent/stale/negative required evidence, `DOWNGRADED` for important gaps, `READY` otherwise. Optional gaps are reported without a severe penalty. ITF tennis events prioritize ITF rather than ATP/WTA.

The API research planner includes the relevant required fields and priority sources in its search plan. `GET /v1/admin/research/route/:lineId` exposes the policy, selected evidence IDs, evidence-to-component effects and deterministic analysis for an existing board line. This is a read-only explanation, not a new paid research run.

The 26 cases in `packages/engine/test/fixtures/research/` represent the user's 24 worked examples plus explicit NHL goalie-save and KBO batter/pitcher fantasy variants. They use fictional/synthetic observations even when a real player name is attached. Fields omitted from `evidenceFound` remain missing; the test helper does not fill them. Therefore several partial examples intentionally produce `PASS` or `DOWNGRADED` until required/important evidence arrives. The examples are not live projections, player-performance claims or calibrated recommendations.

`packages/engine/test/research-registry.test.ts` checks source choice, TTL, component mappings, official-source priority, ITF failover, absence of fabricated metrics, optional/important/required distinctions, fantasy scoring, deterministic AI-versus-GKR precedence, CS2 danger zone and an alternate-line ladder. Numeric component totals in the examples describe expected synthetic reasoning, not automatic assignments by the research router; only the deterministic GKR scorer can assign line scores. A research policy with `modelVersion: null` is research-ready but not a supported calibrated scoring market; its final analysis remains `PASS / MODEL_SUPPORT_INCOMPLETE`.

The expensive full-board web research workflow remains disabled. These tests are offline and do not call OpenAI, search providers or live sporting feeds. Before using any new source as production evidence, wire and verify its actual URL, source identity, event/player match, timestamp, and rights/availability; do not promote a suggested source label or an AI summary into a verified observation.
